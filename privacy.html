Privacy Policy

10Drinks

1. Introduction

10Drinks ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is an alcohol consumption tracking and personal wellness application designed to help users monitor their drinking habits, track alcohol-free days, reflect on their progress, and develop healthier habits.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use 10Drinks, including when you use premium features and purchase subscriptions through Google Play.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary to provide and improve the App.

2. Information We Handle

A. Information you enter

Depending on how you use the App, information may include:

  • Alcohol consumption records and daily drinking logs
  • Alcohol-free days and streaks
  • Journey start dates and progress history
  • Personal goals, milestones, and habit-tracking information
  • Responses to alcohol-related self-assessment questionnaires
  • Preferences and settings you choose within the App

This information may be sensitive because it relates to personal habits and wellness.

B. Information stored on your device

The App stores your tracking information locally on your device using local storage and related application storage mechanisms.

This information is used to operate the App, calculate progress, display your history, and maintain your journey.

We do not operate our own cloud account system for synchronizing your personal tracking history at this time.

C. Subscription and purchase information

If you purchase or restore a premium subscription, certain purchase-related information is processed by our third-party providers:

  • Google Play processes purchases and subscription payments.
  • RevenueCat helps us manage subscriptions, verify purchase status, and determine whether premium access is active.

Depending on the subscription transaction and SDK configuration, RevenueCat may process information such as app user identifiers, purchase records, product identifiers, subscription status, transaction information, and relevant technical information.

We do not receive or store your full payment card details through the App. Payment processing is handled by Google Play.

3. RevenueCat and Google Play

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and premium entitlement status.

RevenueCat may process purchase-related information to:

  • Validate purchases
  • Determine active subscription status
  • Manage subscription entitlements
  • Support subscription restoration
  • Help maintain premium access across supported devices
  • Provide subscription-related technical services

Google Play independently processes payments and manages your Google Play subscriptions.

These services operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Terms of Service

Your subscription can be managed or cancelled through your Google Play account.

4. Backup and Restore

The App may allow you to export your personal tracking information into a backup file and subsequently import that file to restore your information.

Backup files may contain sensitive personal information, including alcohol consumption records, dates, journey progress, and settings.

You are responsible for securely storing exported backup files and deciding where to share or transfer them.

We do not automatically upload your backup files to our servers.

5. Health and Wellness Disclaimer

10Drinks is intended for personal tracking, habit development, and general wellness support.

The App may provide alcohol-related self-assessment tools, educational information, or general guidance.

10Drinks is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

Assessment results are informational and should not be considered a substitute for evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional.

If you are concerned about alcohol dependence, withdrawal symptoms, or your health, please seek appropriate medical assistance. Stopping alcohol consumption suddenly may be medically dangerous for some people.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal tracking information.

We do not share your alcohol consumption records or self-assessment responses with advertisers.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide specific services, including:

  • RevenueCat for subscription management
  • Google Play for payment and subscription processing

We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or when necessary to protect our legal rights, users, or the security of the App.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your personal tracking data is primarily stored on your device.

You can delete locally stored information by using applicable reset or deletion functionality within the App, or by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

Please note that uninstalling the App may permanently remove locally stored tracking information if you have not created a backup.

Subscription and transaction records maintained by Google Play or RevenueCat are subject to their respective retention policies and legal obligations.

Deleting the App does not automatically cancel an active subscription. You must cancel subscriptions separately through Google Play.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any backup files you create.

9. Children's Privacy

10Drinks is not intended for children under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. International Data Processing

RevenueCat and Google may process information on servers located in different countries, depending on their infrastructure and service operations.

Where applicable, these providers implement safeguards intended to protect information in accordance with relevant privacy laws.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request information about personal data processed about you
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Raise concerns about the handling of your personal information

Because most tracking information is stored locally, you can generally manage it directly through the App.

For subscription and payment information, you may also need to contact Google Play or RevenueCat, depending on the information involved.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services, or legal requirements change.

The revised policy will be published within the App or at its designated privacy policy URL. The updated effective date will appear at the top of the policy.

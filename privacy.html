Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 10 Drinks handles your information.

The App is designed to help you track alcohol-free days, drinking days, Journeys, progress, and related self-assessment information.

1. Your privacy

The App is designed with a local-first approach.

Your personal tracking data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account system or a cloud database for your tracking data.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your alcohol-related information for advertising or profiling.

2. Information stored by the App

Depending on the features you use, the App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Sober and drinking-day records
  • Unlogged days and daily Journey history
  • Journey scores
  • Current and previous Journeys
  • Streak and progress information
  • Calendar and historical records
  • Milestone and achievement progress
  • Reminder settings, if you enable reminders
  • Backup/export information, such as the last export time
  • Local app settings and preferences
  • Local information required to remember your Premium access status, if Premium features are introduced

Some of this information may be considered health-related or sensitive because it can reveal information about your alcohol use.

3. AUDIT-C information

The App includes the AUDIT-C, a three-question alcohol-use screening tool.

Your answers and resulting screening score may be processed temporarily on your device to calculate and display your result.

AUDIT-C information is not intended to be stored as a cloud account record or transmitted to us.

AUDIT-C is a screening tool and is not a diagnosis.

4. How your information is used

Your locally stored information is used only to provide the App's features, including:

  • Tracking your Sober and Drinking days
  • Calculating Journey scores and streaks
  • Showing your Journey history
  • Displaying your Best Scores
  • Showing milestones and progress
  • Providing the calendar and historical view
  • Providing reminders when you choose to enable them
  • Providing the AUDIT-C screening experience
  • Supporting backup and restore features that you initiate

We do not use your alcohol-related tracking information to determine your identity, sell products to you, or provide targeted advertising.

5. Information we do not collect

The App does not require you to create an account.

We do not intentionally collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • Photos
  • Audio recordings
  • Health Connect data
  • Your browsing history
  • Your alcohol-tracking information on our servers

The App does not need access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location to provide its core functionality.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We do not share your alcohol-tracking information or AUDIT-C responses with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

If you voluntarily export your data, the exported file is under your control. You are responsible for where you store, send, or share that exported file.

If future versions introduce services that require data sharing, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

7. Backups and exports

The App may allow you to export your local data for backup purposes.

An exported backup may contain information about your Journeys, drinking days, sober days, scores, streaks, and other app data.

You should treat an exported backup as private because it may contain sensitive information about your alcohol use.

The App does not automatically upload your backup to our servers.

8. Data deletion

You can delete your App data using the deletion controls provided within the App.

You can also uninstall the App to remove its locally stored data, subject to the behavior of your device and operating system.

Because your tracking data is designed to remain on your device rather than in our cloud database, we generally do not have a copy of your local tracking data that we can retrieve or delete on your behalf.

If you export your data, deleting the App does not automatically delete copies of that exported file that you may have saved elsewhere.

9. Data retention

Your tracking information remains on your device until you:

  • delete it using the App's deletion controls,
  • clear the App's data,
  • uninstall the App, or
  • otherwise remove the information from your device.

Temporary AUDIT-C responses are processed on your device for the screening experience and are not intended to be retained as a cloud record.

10. Security

We use a local-first design to reduce unnecessary transmission of sensitive tracking information.

However, no device or software system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on your device and for protecting any exported backup files.

11. Reminders

If you enable reminders, the App may store your selected reminder time locally on your device and use the device's notification functionality to provide the reminder.

You can disable reminders through the App or your device's notification settings.

12. Children's privacy

The App is not intended to encourage alcohol use or to provide alcohol-related services to children.

The App is intended for adults.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Medical and health information

The App provides educational information and self-tracking tools related to alcohol use.

The App is not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical care.

AUDIT-C is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.

If you drink heavily or regularly, or have experienced withdrawal symptoms, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional before making a major reduction or stopping suddenly.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality or data practices change.

When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this policy.

You should review this page periodically for the latest information.

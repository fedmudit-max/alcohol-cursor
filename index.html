A Journey is like a football match.

On a sober day, you score 1 goal.
On a drinking day, the opponent scores 1 goal.

The match ends when 10 goals are scored against you — e.g. 12–10.

The goal is simple: next match, beat your previous score — e.g. 15–10.
Strengthen your ability to resist drinking, one match at a time.

How 10Drinks builds will-power

Every day has two choices

☀️ ✓ I Did Not Drink Today — adds 1 sober day to your Current Journey.

🍺 🥃 I Drank Today — one of the 10 drinking days is used.

Each journey has 10 drinking days. Log as many sober days as you can before 10 drinking days are used. Then this journey ends and a new one starts with 10 drinking days again.

Example — each journey allows up to 10 drinking days. Your score is sober days when the journey ends (e.g. 7–10 means 7 sober days and 10 drinking days used).

Journey 1: 7–10 (7 sober days)

Journey 2: 10–10 — 3 days improvement vs Journey 1

Journey 3: 13–10 — 3 days improvement vs Journey 2

Journey 4: 20–10 — 7 days improvement vs Journey 3

…and so on. Against the benchmark of 10 drinking days, aim to score more sober days than your last journey.

Streak tab — consecutive sober days in a row.
Best Streak — longest ever streak.
Milestone tab — sober days in this journey (10, 15, 20 …).
